Finished another section of *The Design of Everyday Things*.

The useful reminder is that good interfaces don't merely explain what a user can do. They make the intended action obvious through the structure of the thing itself.

That applies surprisingly well to software APIs.

> Make the right action easy to discover before you make the documentation longer.

I'm going to keep this in mind while working on the component APIs in this project.
