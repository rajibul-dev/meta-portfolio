# Twitch Timestamp Grabber

Twitch Timestamp Grabber is a focused utility built around a simple problem: getting useful timestamps out of long video sessions without manually dealing with unnecessary friction.

The interesting part of the project was keeping the workflow small. Instead of building a large application around the problem, the project focuses on making one repetitive task quick and straightforward.

It is the kind of project I enjoy because a small tool can sometimes be more useful than a much larger piece of software.
