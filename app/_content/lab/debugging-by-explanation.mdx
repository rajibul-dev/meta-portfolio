Something I keep coming back to: explaining a bug out loud is often more useful than staring harder at the code.

The explanation forces the mental model into a sequence.

"This function receives X, transforms it into Y, and then passes Y to Z."

Usually there is a moment where that sentence stops being true.

That's often where the bug is hiding.
