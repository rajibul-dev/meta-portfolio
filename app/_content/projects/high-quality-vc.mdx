# High Quality VC

High Quality VC is an experiment around making voice communication sound better without making the interface or workflow more complicated.

The project became an opportunity to explore audio processing, signal characteristics, and the practical difference between something that is technically measurable and something that actually sounds better to a person.

It also reflects one of the things I like about software: a seemingly simple application can lead into surprisingly deep technical territory once you start asking how the underlying system actually works.
