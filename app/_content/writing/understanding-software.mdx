# What I Actually Mean by Understanding Software

I have become increasingly interested in what sits underneath the frameworks and abstractions I use every day.

There is something different about building a small server yourself, experimenting with a protocol, or trying to understand what a rendering system is actually doing. You start seeing software as a collection of systems rather than just a collection of frameworks.

This is a short reflection on why that matters to the way I learn and what I want to become capable of building.
