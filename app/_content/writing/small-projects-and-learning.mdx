# Why Small Projects Keep Me Learning

I have never been particularly good at staying interested in something when learning it feels completely disconnected from making something.

A small project gives a concept somewhere to go. It turns a programming language, a protocol, a graphics API, or an unfamiliar tool into another ability I can actually use.

That is one reason I keep coming back to experiments. They do not always become products, but they often leave me with a better understanding of what I can build next.
